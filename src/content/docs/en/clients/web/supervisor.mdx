The supervisor interface (`/supervisor/*`) is centered on controlling teller counter cash flows, verifying daily reports, and overseeing the school's central bank vault.

## 1. Operational Dashboard (`/supervisor`)

Provides real-time visibility across branch operations:
- Metric cards: currently open shifts, reports awaiting review (`submitted`), and reports on hold (`on_hold`).
- Total live cash held across active teller drawers versus central vault holdings.
- Quick navigation to review queues and vault transfers.

## 2. Reviewing Daily Reports (`/supervisor/reports`)

Lists all teller shift records (`GET /api/supervisor/daily-reports`):
- Filter options: All, Awaiting Review (`submitted`), On Hold (`on_hold`), Approved (`approved`), and Rejected (`rejected`).
- Date picker filter.
- Columns detail teller name, date, opening cash, transaction volume, system balance, reported physical cash, and cash discrepancy.
- Rows with discrepancies highlight differences in yellow or red alert badges.

## 3. Report Review Detail (`/supervisor/reports/:id`)

Clicking a shift row navigates to the detailed audit view:

<Steps>

1. **Denomination Breakdown:** Compares physical opening cash counts against closing cash counts.
2. **Shift Transactions:** Displays all deposits, withdrawals, and supervisor-authorized corrections executed during the shift.
3. **Supervisor Decision (Action Panel):**
   - **Approve:** Submits `POST /api/supervisor/daily-reports/{id}/approve`. Status transitions to `approved`. The backend immediately triggers an atomic cash transfer from the teller's drawer to the main vault (`cash_transfers`).
   - **Hold:** Submits `POST .../hold`. Used when the supervisor needs to investigate a physical discrepancy without invalidating the shift. Supervisor notes are mandatory.
   - **Reject:** Submits `POST .../reject`. Status becomes `rejected`. The teller must recount their physical cash, update counts, and resubmit. Supervisor explanation notes are required.
4. **PDF Export:** Download the formal signed daily report PDF via `GET /api/supervisor/daily-reports/{id}/export`.

</Steps>

## 4. Main Vault Ledger (`/supervisor/vault`)

Tracks physical cash custody transfers into the school's central safe:
- Sourced from `GET /api/supervisor/cash-transfers`.
- Every entry originates from an `approved` teller daily report.
- Details transfer timestamp, report ID, depositor teller, transfer amount, and approving supervisor.
- Cumulative vault balance is tallied automatically from historical transfers.

## 5. PIN Reset Requests Monitoring (`/pin-reset-requests`)

Supervisors have read-only visibility into the list of self-service PIN reset requests initiated by mobile customers:
- Monitors pending and approved requests across all customers.
- Reviews admin decision notes and completion timestamps for operational compliance.
- Decision action buttons (Approve/Reject) are restricted to administrators and hidden or disabled for supervisors.
