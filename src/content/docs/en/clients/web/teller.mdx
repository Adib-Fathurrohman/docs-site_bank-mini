The teller interface (`/teller/*`) is designed for day-to-day school bank counter operations. All money-handling endpoints are protected by an active shift gate.

## 1. Opening a Shift (`/teller`)

Before serving students at the counter, tellers must open today's shift:

<Steps>

1. Open `/teller`. If no shift is open today, the **Open Shift** form is presented automatically.
2. Count the opening physical drawer cash using the cash denomination table (`DenominationCounter`):
   - Enter counts for each Indonesian rupiah denomination (Rp100,000, Rp50,000, ..., Rp100).
   - The system calculates the opening cash balance in real time.
3. Click **Open Shift**, submitting `POST /api/teller/shift/open`.
4. Once opened, the navbar pill turns green (`Open Shift`), unlocking the counter routes.

</Steps>

## 2. Service Counter (`/teller/counter`)

The counter page is the primary operational hub.

### Customer lookup
- **Webcam QR Scanning:** Using `QrScanner`, hold a student ID card or mobile digital card in front of the camera. The `BANKMINI:{account_number}` payload is decoded instantly.
- **Manual Search:** Tellers can type the student's NIS or account number directly into the search bar.
- Upon retrieval, the `CustomerPanel` displays student identity, class, major, live balance, account status, and app activation status.

### Cash deposit
1. Select the **Deposit** tab.
2. Enter the amount (must be a multiple of Rp100).
3. Optionally add a deposit note.
4. Click **Process Deposit**. The request is dispatched via `POST /api/teller/deposits` with a client-generated `Idempotency-Key` header.
5. The transaction receipt dialog (`TransactionReceipt`) appears with an option to print or save the receipt PDF.

### Cash withdrawal & PIN authorization
Depending on the configured `withdrawal_verification` mode:

- **Mode A (Mobile App Approval - Default):**
  1. Enter the withdrawal amount. Pre-checks verify that the remaining balance will satisfy the minimum balance threshold (Rp10,000) and that the student has activated their mobile app.
  2. Click **Request Customer Approval**. The client posts to `POST /api/teller/withdrawal-authorizations`.
  3. The `WithdrawalApproval` polling modal opens, counting down 120 seconds while checking status every 2 seconds.
  4. The student approves the prompt on their personal smartphone by entering their PIN.
  5. Once the status transitions to `approved`, the teller clicks **Complete Withdrawal** (`POST .../complete`) and disburses cash.
- **Mode B (Counter Terminal PIN):**
  - If terminal mode is enabled, the customer enters their 6-digit PIN on the teller's screen before the teller dispatches `POST /api/teller/withdrawals`.

## 3. Transactions & Corrections (`/teller/transactions`)

Lists all transactions recorded by the teller during the current shift.

- **Reprint Receipt:** Click the receipt icon on any row to open the receipt preview and download the PDF.
- **Transaction Correction:**
  - If an error was made in amount or account, click **Correct**.
  - The `CorrectionDialog` requires an active **Supervisor** on-site to input their supervisor username and password.
  - A descriptive explanation (minimum 10 characters) is required.
  - The request calls `POST /api/teller/transactions/{id}/correct`, creating an immutable reversal transaction.

## 4. Closing the Shift (`/teller/close`)

At the conclusion of service hours, tellers reconcile and close their shift:

<Steps>

1. Navigate to **Close Shift**. The page outlines system calculated balances (`system_balance`), total deposits, and total withdrawals.
2. Enter the final physical cash counts into the denomination breakdown table.
3. The interface calculates cash discrepancies (`difference = physical_balance - system_balance`).
4. If a difference exists, the teller must explain it in the closing remarks field.
5. Click **Submit Daily Report**, which dispatches `POST /api/teller/shift/close`.
6. Shift status becomes `submitted`, awaiting supervisor review and vault transfer.

</Steps>
