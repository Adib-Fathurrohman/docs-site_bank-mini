Audit screens (`/audit/*` and `/reports`) are accessible by **Administrator** and **Supervisor** roles to inspect financial integrity and produce statutory documents.

## 1. Transaction Audit Trail (`/audit/transactions`)

Provides complete end-to-end traceability for every ledger movement (`GET /api/audit/transactions`):
- **Search Filters:**
  - Date ranges (`from` and `to`, supporting `YYYY-MM-DD` and `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`).
  - Transaction types: Cash deposit (`deposit`), Cash withdrawal (`withdrawal`), Deposit correction (`deposit_correction`), or Withdrawal correction (`withdrawal_correction`).
  - Minimum amount (`min_amount`).
  - Teller selector.
- **Audit Table:** Details timestamp, transaction ID, teller name, student customer name & account number, movement type, amount, and running balance.
- **Transaction Detail (`/audit/transactions/:id`):** Reveals complete audit linkages, including associated daily shift reports, reversal pairs (if corrected), authorizing supervisor signatures, and double-entry journal lines.
- **PDF Export:** An **Export PDF** button downloads the official audit log document.

## 2. Double-Entry General Journal (`/audit/journals`)

Inspects accounting entries generated for financial transactions (`GET /api/audit/journals`):
- Every transaction writes two matching lines: one Debit and one Credit.
- Standard school bank chart of accounts:
  - **101 (Cash):** Debited on deposits, credited on withdrawals.
  - **201 (Customer Savings):** Bank liability to students. Credited on deposits, debited on withdrawals.
- Filter by date range and account code.
- **Export Journal PDF** button downloads the formatted general journal.

## 3. Reports Export Center (`/reports`)

Central hub for generating school financial reports:
- **Bank Mini Annual Report (`GET /api/reports/annual`):** Formal annual document detailing executive summaries, total deposits and withdrawals, customer breakdowns per academic major, and balance sheets.
- **Trial Balance:** Verifies equal total debit and credit balances over specified periods.
- **Customer Statement Export:** Generates official account statements for any student (`GET /api/customers/{id}/statement`).
