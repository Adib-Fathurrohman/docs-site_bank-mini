**E-Teller Bank Mini Sekolah** is a school's internal banking system, built as a
software engineering (RPL) project for the UKK competency exam. It manages student
savings: cash deposits and withdrawals at the counter, a double-entry accounting
journal, teller shifts with cash reconciliation, and supervisor review of daily
reports.

## How it works in one paragraph

All money moves through a **teller at the counter**. The teller opens a shift by
counting the cash drawer, records customers' deposits and withdrawals, and closes the
shift by counting the cash again, which must match the system balance. A
**supervisor** reviews that daily report; once approved, the cash is recorded as
moved from the teller's drawer to the main vault. The **administrator** manages staff
accounts, customer accounts and settings. **Customers** (students) see their balance
and statement, and approve or reject a teller-started withdrawal with their PIN in
their app.

<Aside type="note" title="Not a real bank">
The system is not connected to a payment gateway, an outside bank or any third-party
service. There are no transfers between accounts, no loans and no customer-initiated
transactions.
</Aside>

## Four actors

| Actor | Logs in with | In short |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Administrator | Username + password | Manages staff, customer accounts and settings; reads the journal and reports |
| Supervisor | Username + password | Reviews tellers' daily reports, authorizes corrections, monitors the vault and journal |
| Teller | Username + password | Runs a shift, records deposits and withdrawals, corrects mistakes |
| Customer (*nasabah*) | NIS or account number + 6-digit PIN | Views their own account; approves or rejects withdrawals with their PIN |

Details are in [Roles & permissions](/en/concepts/roles-and-permissions/).

## Core principles

- **The backend is the source of truth.** Every request is checked again on the
  server (token, role, record ownership, business rules). Hiding a button in the
  frontend is not security.
- **Transactions are never edited or deleted.** Mistakes are reversed with a
  correction transaction.
- **Every balance change is atomic.** The balance, the transaction and its two journal
  lines are written in one database transaction with row locks.
- **Every withdrawal needs the customer's PIN.** By default the customer approves it
  in their own app, so the teller never sees the PIN.
