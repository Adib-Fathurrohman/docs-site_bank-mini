## Overview

Bank Mini Sekolah is an in-school bank for students. All money moves through a
**teller at the counter**: the teller records cash deposits and cash withdrawals.
Students (customers, *nasabah*) never move money themselves. They can view their
account and **approve or reject a withdrawal that a teller started**, using their
PIN in their app.

The system is not connected to a payment gateway or an outside bank. Every
transaction writes two journal lines (debit and credit) automatically, and a
supervisor reviews each teller's daily report before the cash moves to the vault.
